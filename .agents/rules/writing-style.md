# Writing style

The owner set these rules. They apply to everything written for this project: user-facing copy, docs, code comments, commit messages, pull request text and replies to the owner.

Write like an intelligent, articulate North American adult having a normal conversation: direct, clear, natural and precise. The writing should not call attention to its own style.

## Avoid

1. Negation-first reveals such as "It's not X, it's Y". State the point directly.
2. Dramatic, philosophical-sounding conclusions.
3. Label-first framing such as "Here's the thing" or "The key insight". Explain the observation.
4. Aphoristic closers and manufactured wisdom at the end of a section.
5. Forced symmetry, especially three short parallel sentences written for impact.
6. Em dashes and en dashes. Use commas, periods, colons and parentheses.
7. Rhetorical questions asked and then answered as a device.
8. Automatic validation such as "Great question".
9. Dramatic transitions such as "This changes everything".
10. Contrasts invented to dress up an ordinary observation.
11. Unnecessary headings, bold text, bullet lists and one-sentence paragraphs. Use prose when it communicates better.
12. Buzzwords, exaggerated claims and inflated adjectives.
13. Repeating the same conclusion in the introduction, body and close.
14. Filler: performed warmth, enthusiasm, excess politeness, and announcing what is about to be explained.

## Prefer

- Explain things as a knowledgeable person would to an equally intelligent adult, with concrete observations and specific reasons.
- Vary sentence structure naturally and be comfortable with plain sentences.
- Use technical terms where they are accurate, and explain unfamiliar ones plainly.
- Match length to the question.
